# 🚀 Water Rocket Engineering Challenge

> **NC State Summer Camp 2026 · Engineering Design Project**

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

During NC State Summer Camp, I designed, built, and launched a water rocket. This project gave me the opportunity to apply **physics, engineering design, and experimental thinking** to a working prototype.

### 🏆 Final Result

**77 feet**

That was the measured height reached by my rocket during the final competition.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Design and build a **stable water rocket** capable of reaching the greatest possible height during the final launch challenge.

---

## 🧰 Materials

* Plastic bottle
* Cork
* Water
* Foam board fins
* Hand pump
* Weight for balancing

---

## 🛠️ Design

I used foam-board fins to help stabilize the rocket during flight. I also placed weight near the top of the rocket to improve its balance.

The bottle contained water and compressed air, which provided the force needed to launch the rocket.

---

## 🔬 Engineering Design Process

| Step           | What I Did                                                                       |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Ask**     | What makes a water rocket fly high while remaining stable?                       |
| **2. Imagine** | Consider how fins, weight, balance, water, and air pressure could affect flight. |
| **3. Plan**    | Select materials and determine the placement of the fins and weight.             |
| **4. Build**   | Construct the rocket from the selected materials.                                |
| **5. Test**    | Launch the prototype and observe its flight.                                     |
| **6. Improve** | Identify variables to test in a future design.                                   |

---

## ⚛️ Physics Connections

### 🚀 Thrust

Water was forced downward during launch, producing an upward reaction force on the rocket.

### 💨 Velocity

The rocket's velocity changed throughout its flight as it accelerated upward and then slowed because of gravity and air resistance.

### ⚡ Kinetic Energy

While the rocket was moving, it possessed kinetic energy.

### 🏔️ Gravitational Potential Energy

As the rocket gained altitude, its gravitational potential energy increased.

### 📐 Stability

The fins, mass distribution, and balance of the rocket influenced how straight its flight path was.

---

## 🏆 Final Result

### **77 FT**

The rocket reached a measured height of **77 feet** during the final competition.

---

## 🔧 What I Would Test Next

For a second version, I would change **one variable at a time** and record the resulting launch height.

| Variable        | Question                                       |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 💧 Water volume | What amount produces the best flight?          |
| 📐 Fin shape    | Does a different fin design improve stability? |
| ⚖️ Nose weight  | How does mass distribution affect flight?      |
| 🪶 Overall mass | How does total mass affect maximum height?     |

A controlled testing process would make it easier to determine which design changes actually improve performance.

---

## 💡 What I Learned

This project taught me that successful engineering isn't just about building something once. **Testing, measuring, and improving the design are just as important as the original idea.**

I also learned how concepts from physics can directly affect the performance of a real engineering project.
