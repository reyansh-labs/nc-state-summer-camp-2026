# Day 3 – Biomanufacturing & AI in Industry

## Activities

- Visited NC State's Biomanufacturing Training and Education Center (BTEC).
- Explored how biological materials can be used to manufacture medicines, vaccines, and other biotechnology products.
- Attended a presentation from Trane Technologies about artificial intelligence in modern industry.
- Learned how AI can help organizations improve efficiency, reduce energy use, and work toward carbon-reduction goals.
- Explored BrainBox AI, a system that optimizes heating and cooling systems.
- Learned about data centers and ARIA, an AI technology used to support operations and decision-making.

## What I Learned

I learned that biomanufacturing combines biology, engineering, and technology to produce useful products on a large scale. The visit helped me see that developing a medicine or vaccine involves more than scientific discovery—it also requires carefully designed and controlled manufacturing processes.

I also learned how AI can help buildings use energy more efficiently. Systems such as BrainBox AI can analyze information and adjust heating and cooling operations, which may reduce energy consumption, emissions, and operating costs.

## My Favorite Part

My favorite part was visiting BTEC because I saw how biotechnology is applied in real-world manufacturing. I also enjoyed learning how AI can help create smarter and more sustainable buildings.

## My Reflection

This day showed me that engineering often connects several fields. Biology, manufacturing, computer science, and environmental responsibility can all contribute to one solution.
