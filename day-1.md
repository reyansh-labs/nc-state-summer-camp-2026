# Day 1 – AI Ethics & DNA Exploration

## Activities

- Discussed the ethical and responsible use of artificial intelligence.
- Explored different types of AI and how they are used to solve real-world problems.
- Completed a hands-on DNA extraction laboratory activity.
- Analyzed elephant DNA to identify genetic relationships and explore how genetics can support wildlife conservation.

## What I Learned

I learned that AI can be a powerful tool when it is used responsibly and ethically. I gained a better understanding of how AI is applied in healthcare, education, engineering, and everyday technology.

I also learned how scientists extract DNA from cells and use DNA analysis to investigate relationships between elephant populations. This showed me how genetics can support biological research and conservation.

## My Favorite Part

My favorite part was the DNA extraction activity because I had the opportunity to perform a scientific experiment. I also enjoyed connecting elephant DNA analysis to the protection of endangered species.

## My Reflection

This activity showed me that computer science and biology can work together. AI can help researchers study large amounts of information, while DNA provides evidence that scientists can use to understand and protect animal populations.
